Early life and education

Lütfü Haluk Bayraktar was born in Istanbul on 4 October 1978, the son of Özdemir Bayraktar (1949–2021), a mechanical engineer who founded Baykar in 1984 as an automotive parts manufacturer, and Canan Bayraktar. He grew up around machine tools, drawings and late nights in his father's workshop, an upbringing that he and his brothers Selçuk and Ahmet describe as their real school.

He earned his bachelor's degree in Industrial Engineering at the Middle East Technical University (METU) in Ankara in 2000, and a master's degree in Financial Engineering from the Industrial Engineering and Operations Research department of Columbia University in New York in 2002. After returning to Türkiye he began doctoral studies at Boğaziçi University in 2004, alongside his work at Baykar.

Building Baykar

Haluk Bayraktar joined Baykar in 2004 as engineering manager, at a time when the company was still an automotive supplier and its work on unmanned aircraft was only beginning. He took part in every stage of the product life cycle of Türkiye's first national unmanned aerial systems, from conceptual design and prototyping to testing, production and operator training, and later led project management, logistics support and business development. He has served as the company's general manager and CEO since January 2010.

Under his leadership Baykar grew from fewer than ten employees into one of Türkiye's leading technology companies, developing a family of platforms that includes the Bayraktar Mini UAV, the Bayraktar TB2, the Bayraktar AKINCI, the Bayraktar TB3 for ship-based operations, and the unmanned fighter jet Bayraktar KIZILELMA, which made its first flight in December 2022. The company is known for financing its research and development with its own resources and for exporting the large majority of what it produces.

Baykar has been Türkiye's leading defence and aerospace exporter for several consecutive years. In 2024 its exports reached US$1.8 billion, and the company has signed export agreements with dozens of countries across Europe, Asia, the Middle East and Africa.

Expansion into Europe

In 2025 Baykar completed the acquisition of Piaggio Aerospace, the historic Italian aircraft manufacturer known for the P.180 Avanti, after approval by the Italian government. Haluk Bayraktar has served as Chairman of the Board of Piaggio Aerospace since July 2025, with the goal of preserving its industrial heritage, relaunching aircraft production and bringing unmanned systems production to Italy.

The same year Baykar and Leonardo, Italy's leading defence group, announced a 50:50 joint venture, LBA Systems, at the Paris Air Show to develop and produce unmanned aerial technologies for European and international markets.

Ukraine and international partnerships

Haluk Bayraktar played a leading role in building strategic industrial cooperation between Türkiye and Ukraine. Ukraine acquired its first Bayraktar TB2 systems in 2019, and Baykar later selected Ukrainian engines for the AKINCI programme. He co-founded and chairs Black Sea Shield, a joint venture between Baykar and Ukraine's state-owned Ukrspecexport that brings together the aerospace and defence sectors of both countries.

After Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, he stated publicly that Baykar had not supplied and would not supply its technology to Russia, and voiced strong support for Ukraine's sovereignty and territorial integrity. When citizens in several European countries crowdfunded the purchase of TB2 aircraft for Ukraine, Baykar donated the aircraft and asked that the money raised be sent to humanitarian aid instead. The Bayraktar TB2 became a widely recognised symbol of Ukrainian resistance.

Technology for the next generation

Alongside his work at Baykar, Haluk Bayraktar co-founded the Turkey Technology Team (T3) Foundation and chaired its board from 2018 to 2022. The foundation's mission is to raise a generation of young people who design and build technology rather than simply consume it.

  • Deneyap Technology Workshops: after-school workshops where secondary school students learn robotics, coding, artificial intelligence, electronics, cyber security and design, now spread across Türkiye.
  • TEKNOFEST: the aviation, space and technology festival first held in Istanbul in 2018. It drew more than 1.7 million visitors in its second year, and by 2026 more than 13.5 million people had attended across Türkiye and abroad. Its technology competitions bring together hundreds of thousands of young people every year.
  • Bilim Üsküdar: Istanbul's first science centre, opened in 2018, whose vision he helped shape.
  • T3 Entrepreneurship Centre and the "1001 Technology Teams" programme, which support young teams in turning their projects into products and companies.

SAHA Istanbul and industrial cooperation

Haluk Bayraktar is a founder and the Chairman of the Board of SAHA Istanbul, the Defence, Aviation and Space Cluster Association, which he has chaired since 2015. Bringing together more than 600 companies and 17 universities, it is the largest industrial cluster in Türkiye. Under his leadership SAHA launched the SAHA EXPO defence and aerospace exhibition, the Industrial Competence Evaluation and Support Programme (EYDEP), a mini MBA for sector leaders, and a range of B2B and innovation network programmes.

At the start of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 he mobilised the cluster to support the national response. Baykar joined Aselsan, Biosys and Arçelik in a project to mass-produce domestically designed intensive care ventilators, the first of which were delivered to the Ministry of Health within about a month. Türkiye later exported and donated these devices to countries in need.

Public service

From 2018 to 2025 Haluk Bayraktar served on the Board of the Scientific and Technological Research Council of Türkiye (TÜBİTAK), where he worked to open the council's resources to small and medium-sized enterprises and to bring its programmes to more schools and public institutions. He is also a co-founder of Cezeri AI, a startup working on civilian artificial intelligence and autonomous systems, and has been listed among Türkiye's highest individual income taxpayers.

State honours

Haluk Bayraktar has received 17 orders and medals from nine countries in recognition of his contributions to their security, technology and bilateral relations. In 2022 he became the first Turkish citizen to receive Ukraine's Order of Merit, First Class, and in 2023 he became the first civilian to receive Kosovo's Medal for Distinguished Service.

Personal

Haluk Bayraktar is a keen fisherman and a frequent guest on Turkish television and at university conferences, where he speaks about national technology, engineering and entrepreneurship. Above all he enjoys spending time with his wife and children.
